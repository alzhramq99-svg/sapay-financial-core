## SApay Demo Support

SApay is currently a demonstration wallet app for testing the user experience. It does not move real money and does not connect to banks or payment networks.

### What can I test?
- Creating a demo account and wallet.
- Viewing simulated balances and activity.
- Simulating transfers between demo wallets.

### Before contacting support
Please include:
- App version and iPhone model/iOS version.
- Approximate date and time of the issue (including time zone).
- Steps that led to the issue.
- Expected behavior and what happened instead.
- A screenshot with email addresses, wallet IDs, tokens, and other personal information hidden.

Never send passwords, one-time codes, authentication tokens, bank details, payment-card details, or identity documents.

### Important demo limitation
Do not use real money or real financial information. All balances and transfers in this version are simulated.
